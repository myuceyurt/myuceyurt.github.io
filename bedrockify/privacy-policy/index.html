Bedrockify Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

Bedrockify converts Minecraft Java world ZIP files to Bedrock world files on your Android device. Contact mertyuceyurt58@gmail.com with privacy questions.

Your worlds and files

You choose a ZIP file through Android's file picker. Bedrockify reads that file and converts it locally; it does not upload your world to our servers. Temporary conversion files are removed when conversion finishes or is cancelled normally; after an unexpected interruption, they may remain until the next conversion or until you clear the app's data. The converted world stays in Bedrockify's private app storage until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. If you save, share, or import a world, copies may remain in the location or app you choose and are subject to that destination's practices. Bedrockify does not create user accounts.

Advertising and privacy choices

Bedrockify uses Google AdMob to request an ad after a successful conversion and Google's User Messaging Platform to manage privacy choices where required. Because children may use the app, all ad requests are marked for child age treatment and limited to general-audience ad content. Personalized advertising and remarketing are disabled. The app does not request access to the Android advertising ID. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may still collect and share your IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, and other device identifiers for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. This data is sent to Google over an encrypted connection. The app does not send the contents of your world to AdMob. You can use the app's Privacy Choices option when it is available. For Google's data practices, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Bedrockify keeps converted worlds only on your device, in its private storage, until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Saved or shared copies are not removed by uninstalling Bedrockify; delete them from their destination separately. Bedrockify has no server-side account or world storage to delete. For questions about data handled by Google, use Google's privacy controls or contact us at the address above.

Changes

We may update this policy if the app's data practices change. The effective date at the top will be updated when we do.

Bedrockify is an independent app and is not an official Minecraft product. It is not approved by or associated with Mojang or Microsoft.
